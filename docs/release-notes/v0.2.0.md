# TVCanvas for Home Assistant 0.2.0

Control each TV from Home Assistant: see what it shows, pick something else, pause, skip and
preview. The README's **Entities** and **Automations** sections have the details and examples.

## New for each TV

- **Now showing**: the item on the TV, by name. Pick another to show it now; the rotation
  carries on from there.
- **Current image**: the image the TV shows, for a picture card.
- **Pause rotation**: the TV keeps its current item and Home Assistant leaves it alone. It
  stays on after a restart.
- **Next image**: shows the next item now.
- **Preview**, **Preview image** and **Show preview on TV**: see an item before sending it.
  Previewing a board renders it and uses 1 credit (sending it to the TV doesn't use another);
  everything else is free.

Images of your phone's photos are shown only to devices on your home network.

## Fixes

- Webcams that send their pictures as a generic download (such as GeoNet's New Zealand volcano
  cams) now work, as long as the file is a JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF.
- Clearer punctuation in two messages (pairing on a Frame, and the Cast local URL error).

## Coming with the next TVCanvas app update

Home Assistant now tells TVCanvas what each TV shows and whether it's paused, so the app can
show it. Until the app and the TVCanvas cloud catch up, everything above works in Home
Assistant on its own.

Update in HACS or Settings → Marketplace: open TVCanvas, select **Update**, then restart Home
Assistant.
