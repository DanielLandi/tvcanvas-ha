# TVCanvas for Home Assistant 0.1.0

First public release. Home Assistant keeps your TVs showing TVCanvas without your phone.

In the TVCanvas iOS app you pin a board, a collection, webcams, a custom image or an art
group to a TV and switch on **Run on Home Assistant**. This integration picks that pin up
from TVCanvas Cloud and keeps the TV updated on its own, day and night, even when your phone
is off or away. You still choose what plays in the app.

## What it does

- **Samsung The Frame** (Art Mode): uploads each image to My Photos with no mat and shows
  it. With *Replace previous* on, it deletes the image it showed before and never touches
  your own art. It waits while the Frame shows TV, runs its own slideshow or is in standby,
  so it uploads nothing and spends no credits then.
- **Google Cast** (Chromecast, Google TV, TVs with Cast built in): Home Assistant serves the
  image on your home network and the TV shows it.
- **Every pin type the app hands over**: boards (rendered by TVCanvas Cloud, using credits
  as on the phone), collections, webcams, custom image links and art groups, each at the
  pin's own interval (1 minute to 24 hours).
- **Photos from your phone**: an art group's photos go from the phone to Home Assistant
  over your home network, never through TVCanvas Cloud. The 12-character code that connects
  Home Assistant also pairs the phone; there is no separate pairing step.
- **Keeps going on its own**: the last pins keep running when TVCanvas Cloud is down, even
  after a restart. Unreachable TVs are retried less and less often and picked up as soon as
  they're back. A Frame or Cast TV that gets a new IP address from your router is found again.
- **One sensor per TV** (*Last update*) with the latest status, and diagnostics that leave
  out your token and your TVs' addresses.

## Requirements

- **Home Assistant 2026.9 or newer.**
- **The TVCanvas app** for iPhone, with Home Assistant support (Settings → Home Assistant),
  signed in to your TVCanvas account.
- **A Samsung The Frame or a Google Cast device** on the same network as Home Assistant.
  Tested on the 2022 Frame; 2024 and newer Frames changed their Art Mode interface and are
  best effort.
- Home Assistant must reach TVCanvas Cloud (`api.tvcanvas.app`) over the internet.

## Install

**Home Assistant 2026.11 or newer:** open the
[TVCanvas repository in your Home Assistant](https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/hacs_repository/?owner=DanielLandi&repository=tvcanvas-ha&category=integration).
Settings → Marketplace offers to add it, then select **Download**.

**Home Assistant 2026.9 or 2026.10:** install [HACS](https://www.hacs.xyz/docs/use/download/download/)
first, then use the same link (or HACS → ⋮ → **Custom repositories** →
`https://github.com/DanielLandi/tvcanvas-ha`, type **Integration**), select **Download** and
restart Home Assistant.

**Manually:** download `Source code (zip)` below and copy `custom_components/tvcanvas` into
your configuration folder, then restart.

Then connect: in the app, **Settings → Home Assistant → Connect Home Assistant**; in Home
Assistant, **Settings → Devices & services → Add integration → TVCanvas**, and type the code
the app shows. Finally, switch on **Run on Home Assistant** on a TV's pin. The
[README](https://github.com/DanielLandi/tvcanvas-ha#readme) has the details and a
troubleshooting table.

## Privacy

- **To TVCanvas Cloud** Home Assistant sends only the pairing code (its first 8 characters),
  a check for changed pins every minute, and for each refresh a short status (success or
  failure, the time). Never your TVs' IP addresses or other details of your network, and
  never the images shown or anything else from Home Assistant.
- **Your phone's photos** go from the phone to Home Assistant on your home network, and from
  Home Assistant only to a TV on your home network. Home Assistant refuses any TV address
  that isn't private or link-local. The photos are stored in `config/tvcanvas/images/` and
  are part of Home Assistant's backups.
- **Webcams and custom links** are fetched by Home Assistant directly, only from public
  `https://` addresses; passwords and API keys in a link are left out of logs and
  diagnostics.
- Removing the integration deletes its stored pins, Frame permissions, paired phones' keys
  and photos.

## Known limits

- Collages, picture-in-picture, photo-info overlays and abstract art are drawn by the phone;
  Home Assistant shows the pin's images without them.
- Photos albums (as opposed to an art group's photos) stay on the phone.
- A Cast TV must be able to reach Home Assistant's local `http://` address (Settings →
  System → Network).
